# Realistic impossible-world and festival modes

## Photoreal impossible world

Start with a familiar adult-scale threshold: a station exit, office lift, parking ramp, roadside rest stop, harbour, apartment corridor, or quiet town street. Add exactly one spatial or physical exception, and make it prove itself inside a still frame.

```text
Create an original vertical 9:16 realistic travel photograph of [FAMILIAR PLACE] at [TIME/WEATHER]. One impossible rule: [SINGLE RULE]. Static proof: [A CONTINUOUS ROAD / DRAIN / TILE GRID / SHORELINE / SHADOW / BUILDING EDGE visibly obeys that rule]. The rest of the world remains ordinary, adult-scale, and physically detailed. Natural restrained colour, no neon, no portal frame, no people, no text, logo, brand, watermark, or second impossible event.
```

Reject the concept if a 360×640 blind view can mainly be read as a flood, ordinary mirror, aquarium, split screen, or surreal colour effect rather than the intended rule.

## Seasonal romantic landscape

Treat romance as shared space rather than a couple on screen: two chairs, two cups, paired windows, a table set for two, an empty train berth, or a porch light left on.

```text
Create an original vertical 9:16 warm, realistic landscape for [SEASON OR OCCASION]. No people, faces, silhouettes, hearts, red thread, text, or festival poster graphics. Show [ONE PAIRED LIFE TRACE] in [REAL PLACE], with [WEATHER/LIGHT]. The atmosphere is tender and quiet, not sugary. No brand, logo, watermark, impossible architecture, or purple-blue fantasy glow.
```

The first image must read as healing and inviting before the viewer needs a caption. Do not add a visible relationship rule unless the batch specifically tests one.
