# Series seed library

Use these seeds to build coherent 6-image adult scenery carousels. Recombine the demand, geography, rule, and proof cue; do not repeatedly reproduce the same six scenes.

| Seed | Viewer need | Credible base | One impossible rule | Required single-frame proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Migrating settlement | regional wonder | Chinese mountain or canal town | the whole settlement changes location with one natural force | empty former foundations plus one continuous route to the intact town |
| Quiet threshold | decompression after work | modest library, room, terrace, or station | noise cannot cross one threshold | the same air, rain, fog, or loose objects are disturbed on one side and still on the other |
| Notification release | relief from digital overload | ordinary lived-in apartment | notifications become a natural phenomenon and leave | geometric fragments transform continuously at one opening into leaves, fireflies, rain, or birds; no readable UI |
| Two times, one place | meaningful choice | one continuous coast, valley, street, or bridge | two time or light states coexist | the same landmark, material, and path cross a seamless physical boundary; no graphic split screen |
| Impossible commute | escape from commuting | believable transit and human-scale cities | one route crosses an otherwise impossible environmental boundary | one continuous track and one vehicle caught halfway across air/water/cloud/rock states |
| Recovered time | rest and identity | adult home or public place | consumed time becomes usable physical space | one continuous material transfer from the source of pressure to the restorative space |

## Batch composition

For a six-image traffic test, prefer:

- two place-led scenes for immediate visual novelty;
- two adult-pressure scenes for emotional identification;
- two choice or boundary scenes for comments.

Hold the provider, aspect ratio, palette, realism level, absence of people, and caption format constant. Vary the demand category only. Put the strongest high-contrast boundary scene first; alternate wide exterior and intimate interior scenes to avoid six similar thumbnails.

## Fantasy-intensity gate

For a carousel explicitly framed as “choose an impossible world,” the impossible rule must dominate the thumbnail at 0.5 seconds. A realistic landscape with a small magical detail is insufficient even if the caption explains it.

Acceptable dominant patterns include:

- one continuous river or road bending into an impossible direction;
- a complete city whose gravity or orientation follows a ring, sphere, or wall;
- the same matter crossing a clearly impossible air/water/time boundary;
- a real sky, ocean, desert, or mountain being continuously transferred into a physical container;
- a familiar environment placed beneath a coherent inverted natural system.

Reject when the scene could pass as an ordinary photograph at thumbnail size, when the fantasy cue is only a few particles or lights, or when the viewer must read the caption before noticing anything impossible.

## Validated 2026-08-18 example

The first sequence used: permanent day/night coast → sky-to-sea rail → notification fireflies → sunset-migrating stone town → noise-stopping library → river-migrating canal town. All six passed deterministic portrait/nonblank/clipping QA, but user review found images 3–6 too weak for the “choose an impossible world” promise. The accepted correction replaced them with an upward river, radial-gravity ring city, library that stores the real sky, and desert beneath an inverted ocean. Machine QA cannot detect this fantasy-intensity mismatch; user review is the controlling evidence.
