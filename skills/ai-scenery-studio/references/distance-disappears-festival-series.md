# Distance-disappears festival series

Use this pattern when a festival or life moment is fundamentally about separation and reunion. The visual premise is:

> A believable place keeps its real materials and scale, while exactly one spatial rule makes distance briefly stop working.

This supports romance, family, friendship, graduation, homecoming, and long-distance life. Do not narrow it to couples unless the user asks.

## Decision rule

Keep the emotional theme, rendering style, aspect ratio, absence of recognizable faces, palette, and caption treatment constant. Change only **how distance disappears**. The impossible mechanism must dominate the thumbnail and have one continuous physical proof in the still frame.

Useful mechanisms:

- **Fold:** one continuous road, coastline, wall, or valley folds so two distant places meet; texture and wear cross the crease.
- **Material bridge:** one ordinary natural material becomes a road across a gap through a visible half-transformed section.
- **Shared route across states:** the same train, road, river, or bridge continues through two times, seasons, climates, or gravities.
- **Nature yields:** water, fog, rock, or wind opens one physically legible passage, with residue and boundary evidence.
- **Convergence:** several ordinary paths or flows violate one rule and visibly meet at one destination.
- **Shared object:** one table, room, window, or courtyard exists across two distant lived spaces; material grain crosses the seam.

Do not combine two mechanisms in one image.

## Prompt shape

```text
Create an original vertical 9:16 photorealistic cinematic scenery image for adult short-video viewers.

BELIEVABLE BASE
[Real geography or lived space, weather, architecture, materials, and human scale.]

ONE IMPOSSIBLE RULE
Distance disappears because [one mechanism only].

STATIC PROOF — MUST BE VISIBLE
[One continuous road/material/object/boundary with physical traces crossing the impossible change.] The rule must be readable without captions or animation.

COMPOSITION
One focal landmark; strong foreground, middle ground, and background. Keep interface-safe areas quiet. Make the impossible mechanism dominate the thumbnail.

MOOD
Adult, restrained, emotionally specific, and naturalistic. Use sparse amber warmth against mineral charcoal, mist jade, warm stone, and pale daylight.

AVOID
No readable text, logo, watermark, recognizable face, artist or franchise imitation, heart shape, red thread, giant moon, decorative birds, purple-cyan neon, anime poster, game splash art, random particles, repeated structures, melted edges, or contradictory shadows.
```

## Six-image sequencing

For a carousel, prefer:

1. A large place-led fold or boundary that reads in 0.5 seconds.
2. A culturally legible but non-literal festival image.
3. An adult-life route such as rail, road, commute, or time-zone crossing.
4. A contrasting daylight or green landscape.
5. A second large-scale passage.
6. An intimate lived-space image that turns spectacle into a concrete wish.

Keep place, weather, and mechanism distinct so six thumbnails do not collapse into the same blue night scene.

## Rejection rules

Reject when:

- the relationship is communicated only by a caption or two decorative props;
- the still cannot prove the mechanism without implied motion;
- a spatial seam looks like a mirror, screen, graphic split, or glowing portal;
- a material transition is hidden by fog or bloom;
- the scene reads as a generic festival poster, AI wallpaper, or ordinary photograph at thumbnail size;
- festival clichés become a second visual rule.

Record the failed mechanism and evidence gap. Revise only the proof cue before changing the whole subject.
